## What this says

1. **You found something on 18 July, then reach fell.** That Reel hit almost 9x
   your normal, but the August–October Reels dropped from about 1.4k to 543 plays.
   The full frame-by-frame breakdown is in `video-analysis.md`.
2. **The 11 July Reel got 47 comments**, about 10x your usual. Something in it
   made people want to answer. Whatever the question or debate was, reuse it.
3. **July was clearly better than May and June.** All 4 Reels above 1.4x are
   from July. You were improving, so build on that version of you.
4. **Ideal length is about 25–47 seconds.** Your 91-second Reel and your
   10-second one both landed under the median.
5. **Captions are empty or one word.** That's fine for skits, but it's free
   reach left on the table: Instagram search reads captions now. Add one
   searchable line, e.g. "career switch from architecture to AI".
6. **The account has two personalities.** Funny Reels now, quiet reflective
   photos from before. That's an asset. The reflective side is where the
   "inspire others" content lives.
